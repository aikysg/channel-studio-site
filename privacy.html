Privacy Policy – Channel Studio

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Channel Studio ("the tools") is a set of private desktop tools used by a single person (the owner) to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. The tools use YouTube API Services.

Information the tools access

  • OAuth tokens for the owner's own YouTube channels, granted by the owner through Google sign-in (scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube).
  • Metadata and public statistics of the owner's own channels and videos (titles, publish times, subscriber, view, like and comment counts).

How the information is used, stored and shared

The information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to review their performance. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own PC. A short summary of the owner's own channel statistics is sent to the owner's own private Telegram channel. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with or disclosed to anyone else. No cookies or tracking are used.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the local token files removes all stored credentials; local statistics can be deleted at any time.

Third-party policies

The tools use YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
