Channel Studio

Channel Studio is a set of private, single-user desktop tools. They run only on the owner's PC and manage the owner's own YouTube channels. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

What the tools do with YouTube (YouTube Data API v3)

  • Upload the owner's finished videos to the owner's own channels and schedule their publish time.
  • Set each video's title, description, tags, "made for kids" setting and altered/synthetic content disclosure.
  • Read basic public statistics of the owner's own channels and videos (subscribers, views, likes, comments) to show them on a local dashboard on the owner's PC and to send a short daily summary to the owner's own private Telegram channel.

The tools do not access anyone else's channels and do not share YouTube data with anyone other than the owner.

By using these tools, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
